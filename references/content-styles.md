# Discord styles by content type

How successful servers differ by what the creator makes. Read only the section(s) that match the
community; for a mix (e.g. "AI tutorials + gaming"), take the base of the main niche and borrow
the signature channels of the second.

## 1. Reading a creator's content

Before designing, find these six things - from what the user says, or from their channel page,
recent titles, bio, website or an existing server (`inspect`):

| Signal | Where to look | What it changes |
|---|---|---|
| **Niche** | video titles, channel description | which playbook below |
| **Recurring series / formats** ("Weekly build", "Rank my setup", "Tier list Tuesday") | playlists, repeated title patterns | give each big series its own channel or forum - members discuss episodes there |
| **What fans make or do** (fan art, builds, clips, recipes, workouts) | comments, community posts | a showcase/submission channel shaped for that output |
| **Tone** (hype, calm, educational, comedic, premium) | thumbnails, titles, how they talk | naming style, emoji density, welcome message voice |
| **Audience age** | topic, platform (TikTok/Roblox skew young) | stricter rules and safety for under-18 audiences |
| **Money model** (memberships, Patreon, courses, products, sponsors) | links in bio/description | private tier categories, Customer/Supporter roles |

Use the creator's own vocabulary: if fans are called "Chefs", "Builders" or "the Squad", name the
member role and welcome message that way. Inside jokes and series names in channel names make a
server feel like *theirs*, not a template.

If you can't access their content, ask one question: "What do you post, and what do your viewers
like to do in the comments?"

## 2. Naming styles

Pick one and use it consistently - mixing styles looks messy.

| Style | Example | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Plain | `general`, `announcements` | professional, business, education |
| Emoji prefix | `💬・general`, `📢・announcements` | creators, lifestyle, most communities |
| Bar separator | `🎮┃lfg`, `🏆┃tournaments` | gaming, anime, hype communities |
| Lowercase words | `the-kitchen`, `taste-test` | cozy, niche, personality-driven |

Category names are usually UPPERCASE with one emoji (`🎮 GAMING`). Voice channel names keep
capitals and spaces (`Chill Lounge`). Keep names short - long names get cut off on phones.

## 3. Niche playbooks

Each playbook: **vibe**, **signature channels** (what makes it feel right for that niche, on top of
the usual START HERE / COMMUNITY / STAFF), **roles**, **rituals** (recurring things that keep
people coming back - mention them in the welcome post), **niche rules**, and **bots** (the user
adds these themselves afterwards; suggest them in the final checklist).

### Gaming
- **Vibe**: hype, fast chat, lots of voice. Bar-separator naming.
- **Signature**: `lfg` (looking for group), per-game channels or forum if they play several games,
  `clips-and-highlights`, `setups`, `patch-notes` (read-only), `tournaments`, voice: several
  `Squad 1/2/3` (user limit 4-5), `Chill Lounge`, `Stream Watch`.
- **Roles**: platform/game interest roles (`PC`, `Console`, `Mobile`, per-game) for pinging LFG;
  `Tournament Player`; `Subscriber/Member` tier; `Clip of the Week` winner.
- **Rituals**: weekly game night, monthly tournament, clip of the week.
- **Rules**: no cheating/hacks/account selling, no toxic trash talk, spoilers tagged.
- **Bots**: leveling/XP bot, LFG bot, Twitch/YouTube live alerts.

### Streaming / VTuber
- **Vibe**: centred on going live; fans are "the chat". Emoji or bar naming.
- **Signature**: `live-now` (read-only, alerts), `stream-schedule` (read-only), `clips`,
  `fan-art`, `emote-suggestions`, `stream-ideas`, voice: `Watch Party`.
- **Roles**: `Subscriber` / `Member` tiers matching Twitch/YouTube tiers, `Mod`, `Clipper`,
  `Artist`, notification role (`Live Ping`) so only opted-in people get pinged.
- **Rituals**: schedule post every Monday, fan art feature, sub-only game nights.
- **Rules**: no backseating, no doxxing or real-life speculation (important for VTubers), credit artists.
- **Bots**: live-alert bot, reaction-role bot (self-assign `Live Ping`).

### Music / Producers
- **Vibe**: share-and-feedback culture, creative. Lowercase or emoji naming.
- **Signature**: `share-your-tracks` (slowmode), `feedback-exchange` forum (give feedback to get
  feedback), `collabs`, `samples-and-presets` (read-only for creator drops), `gear-and-setups`,
  `daw-help` (FL / Ableton / Logic), voice: `Listening Party` (stage if Community on), `Studio`.
- **Roles**: DAW roles, genre roles, `Collab Open`, `Producer` / `Vocalist` / `Engineer`.
- **Rituals**: monthly beat challenge with a sample pack, listening parties, feedback Fridays.
- **Rules**: only share music you own, no follow-for-follow, feedback must be specific.

### Art / Design / Photography
- **Vibe**: calm, supportive, visual. Emoji naming.
- **Signature**: `showcase` (slowmode, no chat - discussion in threads), `wip-and-critique` forum,
  `daily-sketch` / `prompt-of-the-week`, `resources-and-brushes`, `commissions` (rules pinned),
  `inspiration`, voice: `Draw Together`.
- **Roles**: medium roles (`Digital`, `Traditional`, `3D`, `Photo`), `Commissions Open`,
  `Challenge Winner`.
- **Rituals**: weekly prompt, monthly challenge with featured winner, art streams.
- **Rules**: credit original artists, no tracing/reposting others' art, label AI-generated work,
  constructive critique only when asked.

### AI & Tech / Coding
- **Vibe**: helpful, fast-moving, practical. Plain or emoji naming.
- **Signature**: `news-and-releases` (read-only), `help` forum (tags: solved/unsolved),
  `show-what-you-built`, `prompts-and-workflows`, per-tool channels for the creator's main tools,
  `jobs-and-gigs`, `resources` (read-only), voice: `Coworking`, `Office Hours`.
- **Roles**: skill-level roles (`Beginner`, `Builder`, `Pro`), tool/language roles, `Helper`
  (members who answer a lot), `Customer` if selling courses/tools/skills.
- **Rituals**: weekly build challenge, office hours, "what are you building" Monday thread.
- **Rules**: share code as text not screenshots, no API keys or passwords in chat (say why: bots
  scrape them), no spam DMs offering services, cite sources for news.

### Business / Finance / Crypto
- **Vibe**: professional, value-dense, trust matters. Plain naming.
- **Signature**: `wins` (accountability), `ask-the-community` forum, `resources` (read-only),
  `accountability-partners`, `market-chat` (finance), `deals-and-tools`, voice: `Mastermind`.
- **Roles**: stage roles (`Starting`, `$1k/mo`, `$10k/mo`) or industry roles, `Member` tier,
  `Coach`/`Mentor`.
- **Rituals**: weekly goals thread, monthly mastermind call, win of the week.
- **Rules**: **no financial advice or signals** presented as advice, no promoting coins/schemes,
  no DM pitching, **staff will never DM you first** (pin this - scams are rampant here).
  For crypto, add `scam-alerts` (read-only) and suggest strict verification.

### Education / Study / Course
- **Vibe**: focused, encouraging. Plain or emoji naming. (See `study-group.json`.)
- **Signature**: per-module or per-subject channels, `ask-a-question` forum, `resources`
  (read-only), `daily-goals`, `wins`, `study-buddies`, voice: silent focus rooms + discussion room.
- **Roles**: cohort/batch roles, `Mentor`/`TA`, exam-target roles.
- **Rituals**: weekly live session, study streaks, exam countdown.
- **Rules**: no doing others' graded work, no pirated materials.

### Fitness / Health
- **Vibe**: motivating, accountability-heavy. Emoji naming.
- **Signature**: `daily-check-in`, `progress-pics` (opt-in, could be private to members),
  `workout-logs`, `nutrition-and-recipes`, `form-check` forum, `challenges`, voice: `Workout Together`.
- **Roles**: goal roles (`Fat Loss`, `Muscle`, `Running`, `Mobility`), challenge participants, `Coach`.
- **Rituals**: 30-day challenges, Monday goals, Sunday weigh-in/reflection thread.
- **Rules**: no body shaming, **no medical advice - see a professional** for injuries/conditions,
  no selling supplements/steroids.

### Cooking / Food
- **Vibe**: warm, cozy. Lowercase or emoji naming.
- **Signature**: `what-i-cooked` (photos), `recipe-requests`, `recipe-book` forum (one post per
  recipe, tags by cuisine/diet), `kitchen-help`, `cook-along` (follow the latest video),
  `gear-and-ingredients`, voice: `Cook Along`.
- **Roles**: diet roles (`Vegetarian`, `Vegan`, `Halal`, `Gluten-free`), `Home Cook`/`Pro Chef`.
- **Rituals**: weekly cook-along of the newest recipe, monthly ingredient challenge.
- **Rules**: label allergens, credit recipe sources.

### Beauty / Fashion
- **Vibe**: expressive, visual, supportive. Emoji naming.
- **Signature**: `outfit-of-the-day` / `look-of-the-day`, `product-recs`, `dupes`, `ask-for-advice`
  forum, `hauls`, `deals-and-drops`, `skincare-routines`.
- **Roles**: style or skin-type roles, `Deal Alerts` ping role.
- **Rituals**: weekly theme (e.g. "monochrome Monday"), monthly favourites.
- **Rules**: no negative comments on bodies/looks, disclose affiliate links, no medical claims.

### Anime / Fandom
- **Vibe**: playful, meme-heavy, spoiler-sensitive. Bar-separator naming.
- **Signature**: **spoiler policy channels** per show/arc (`spoilers-<show>`), `episode-discussion`
  forum (one post per episode), `memes`, `fan-art`, `recommendations`, `merch-and-figures`,
  voice: `Watch Party`.
- **Roles**: favourite-series roles, `Caught Up` (unlocks spoiler channels via `private_to`).
- **Rituals**: weekly episode threads, seasonal tier lists, watch parties.
- **Rules**: spoilers only in spoiler channels or `||spoiler tags||`, no NSFW, no piracy links.

### Podcast / Commentary
- **Vibe**: conversation about episodes; community as the "live studio audience". Emoji naming.
- **Signature**: `episode-discussion` forum (one post per episode), `topic-suggestions`,
  `ask-the-host` (questions answered on air), `clips`, `hot-takes` (slowmode), voice: `Live Recording` (stage).
- **Roles**: `Patron`/`Member` tiers, `Featured Question` (got read on air).
- **Rituals**: questions collected before each recording, live recording events.
- **Rules**: debate ideas not people, no harassment of guests or other creators.

### Travel / Vlog / Lifestyle
- **Vibe**: friendly, personal. Emoji naming.
- **Signature**: per-region channels or a `destinations` forum, `travel-tips`, `photo-dump`,
  `meetups` (with safety rules), `ask-a-local`, `behind-the-scenes` (members).
- **Roles**: region/home-country roles, `Digital Nomad`.
- **Rules**: meet in public places, no sharing anyone's location or private info.

### Faith / Wellness / Mindfulness
- **Vibe**: calm, respectful, safe. Plain or lowercase naming; few emoji.
- **Signature**: `daily-reflection` (read-only post + thread replies), `prayer-requests` or
  `support` (with clear safety resources pinned), `gratitude`, `reading-group`, voice: `Quiet Room`.
- **Rules**: respect all backgrounds, no debating others' beliefs in support channels,
  **pin crisis-line information** and say staff are not counsellors.

### Kids & Family / young audiences
- Discord requires users to be 13+ (higher in some countries). If the audience is younger,
  say so and suggest a parent community instead.
- For teen audiences: verification level High, AutoMod on, no DMs from server members recommended,
  more moderators, no voice channels without a moderator present, stricter rules on personal info.

## 4. Size and age adjustments

| Situation | Adjust |
|---|---|
| Brand-new / under ~500 members | 10-15 channels; merge niche channels into `general`; add more as chat grows |
| Large audience (10k+) | slowmode on busy channels, forum-based help, more staff roles, ping roles instead of @everyone |
| Paid community only | skip public HELP/COMMUNITY sprawl; most channels private to the paid role |
| Mostly under 18 | see Kids & Family; stricter rules and safety settings |
| Non-English audience | write channel names and all posts in their language; add `english` channel only if needed |
